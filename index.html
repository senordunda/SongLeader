    Song Leader — Digital Hymnal, Pitch Pipe & Worship Songlist Planner

    Song Leader is a free, non-commercial Progressive Web App (PWA) created for Church of Christ song leaders to search hymnals, view public-domain sheet music scans, sound starting pitches with a built-in pitch pipe, plan worship service songlists, and track congregation singing history.

    Supported hymnals include Praise for the Lord, Songs of Faith and Praise, Hymns for Worship, Sacred Selections, Psalms, Hymns, and Spiritual Songs, and Songs of the Church.

    All personal notes, favorites, custom tags, songlists, and singing history work offline on your device, with optional Google Sign-In cloud backup and sync. Review our Privacy Policy for details on data handling and security.

      About This App

      Song Leader is a custom built digital tool for leading worship songs from your congregation's hymn collection. Songs currently under copyright protection do not display sheet music or lyrics. This app is free to use for non-commercial purposes, created by Zack Dunda of the Riverside Church of Christ, with all glory to God.
