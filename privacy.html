Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Song Leader (songleaderapp.com) is a free worship song-leading utility built for church song leaders. Your privacy is important, and the app is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

By default, Song Leader works without an account. Your favorites, custom tags, "Don't Sing" flags, personal notes, key transpositions, custom starting pitches, worship setlists, singing history, and display settings are stored locally in your browser's storage on your device. This local data is never transmitted to any server unless you choose to enable Cloud Backup & Sync.

2. Optional Cloud Backup & Sync (Google Sign-In)

If you choose to sign in with Google under Settings → Cloud Backup & Sync, Song Leader uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to back up and sync your library across your devices. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your Google account email address and unique user identifier (UID) to authenticate your session.
  • Your personal app library data (favorites, custom tags, "Don't Sing" flags, personal notes, transpositions, starting pitch preferences, setlists, singing history, and app settings).

Your synced data is restricted by server-side security rules so only your authenticated account can read or write it. We do not sell, rent, share, or use your data for advertising or analytics profiling.

3. Third-Party Services & External Links

Certain optional features connect directly from your browser to third-party services:

  • Scripture Lookup: KJV and ASV scripture references fetch passage text from bible-api.com. Other translations link out to Bible.com.
  • Hymn Recordings & External Resources: Opening a recording loads an embedded player from youtube-nocookie.com. External links to Hymnary.org and HymnStudiesBlog open in your browser and are governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing and Deleting Your Data

  • Local Data: You can export a JSON copy or clear all local data anytime under Settings → Reset All Personal Data, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Cloud Data: While signed in, you can permanently delete your cloud backup at any time by tapping Delete Cloud Data in Settings, or by emailing help@songleaderapp.com to request full account removal.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Zack Dunda at help@songleaderapp.com.
