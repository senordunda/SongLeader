Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Application: Song Leader (https://songleaderapp.com)

1. Purpose of the Application

Song Leader is a free, non-commercial worship planning and song-leading web application created for church song leaders. It provides a searchable hymnal index, public-domain sheet music viewer, audible pitch pipe, worship service setlist planner, and congregation singing history log.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

Local Device Storage (Default): You can use Song Leader without creating an account or signing in. All user-created preferences—including favorite hymns, custom tags, "Don't Sing" flags, personal notes, key transpositions, starting pitch selections, worship setlists, singing history logs, and display preferences—are stored locally in your web browser's localStorage and cache on your device.

Google Sign-In & Cloud Backup (Optional): If you choose to enable Cloud Backup & Sync under Settings → Cloud Backup & Sync, Song Leader requests basic Google OAuth sign-in scopes (openid, email, and profile) through Google Firebase Authentication to identify your account, and stores your personal Song Leader library JSON backup in Google Cloud Firestore so you can synchronize your own setlists, history, and notes across your personal devices.

  • Authentication Data: Your Google email address, display name, and Firebase unique user ID (UID) are used solely to authenticate your sign-in session and isolate your cloud backup record from all other users.
  • Application Library Data: Your synced setlists, singing history, favorites, custom tags, notes, transpositions, and settings are stored exclusively to provide multi-device backup and restore functionality for you.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Song Leader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request the minimum authentication scopes necessary to sign you in and associate your cloud backup with your account.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data or synced app data with third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • We do not use your data for targeted advertising, marketing profiles, or AI/machine-learning model training.
  • Human access to stored cloud data does not occur unless explicitly requested by you for technical support or required to comply with applicable law.

4. Data Security and Retention

All communication between Song Leader and Firebase services is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Cloud Firestore access is protected by strict server-side security rules ensuring that only the authenticated user matching the account UID can read, write, or delete their backup document. Your cloud backup is retained only as long as you maintain your account or until you delete it.

5. Third-Party Services

Optional features in Song Leader may connect directly from your browser to third-party services when invoked by you:

  • Scripture Lookup: KJV and ASV scripture references retrieve passage text from bible-api.com; other translations open external links on Bible.com.
  • Audio & External Hymn Resources: Playing an optional hymn recording embeds a privacy-enhanced video player from youtube-nocookie.com. External links to Hymnary.org and HymnStudiesBlog open in a new browser tab.

6. Your Rights: Exporting and Deleting Your Data

  • Exporting Data: You may download a complete copy of your data in JSON format at any time via Settings → Export Backup (.json).
  • Deleting Local Data: You may erase all data stored on your device at any time via Settings → Reset All Personal Data.
  • Deleting Cloud Data & Revoking Access: While signed in, you may permanently delete your cloud backup at any time via Settings → Delete Cloud Data. You may also revoke Song Leader's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account Permissions page, or email help@songleaderapp.com to request complete deletion of your authentication record.

7. Contact Information

Song Leader is developed and maintained by Zack Dunda. For privacy questions, support, or data deletion requests, please email help@songleaderapp.com.
