Product Updates

Changelog

Recent enhancements, hymnal database updates, and usability improvements for Song Leader.

Songlist Naming, Search Customization & Quick Favorites

November 26, 2026
  • Renamed worship planning lists from Setlists to Songlists across the interface for clearer church song-leading terminology.
  • Added a setting to completely hide hymns flagged as Don't Sing from the directory and Prev/Next song navigation.
  • Added a setting to hide the Invitation and Lord's Supper quick-filter tabs on the main hymnal search bar.
  • Added press-and-hold (long-press) on any hymn row in the search list to toggle Favorites immediately.

Six-Hymnal Library, Cross-References & Cloud Sync

November 24, 2026
  • Expanded directory support to six a cappella hymnals: Praise for the Lord, Songs of Faith and Praise, Hymns for Worship, Sacred Selections, Praise, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, and Songs of the Church.
  • Added cross-hymnal number matching, starting melody note pitch pipe controls, and YouTube listening links.
  • Added optional Google Sign-In cloud sync for Favorites, Songlists, Singing History, custom tags, and personal notes.
  • Improved Singing History date grouping layout and mobile readability sizing options.
